# nResto Mobile — 45-Second Lead Gen Video Script

**Purpose:** Lead generation for BOLT Fusion Tech  
**Target:** Restaurant & café owners, food service managers  
**Platform:** Social ads (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok), landing page hero  
**Duration:** 45 seconds  
**CTA:** "Get a Demo" / "Start Free Trial" / "Book a Call"

---

## HOOK (0–5 sec) — 5 sec

**Visual:** Busy restaurant floor, server struggling with old tablet or paper. Quick cut to frustrated owner checking a long receipt.

**Text on screen (bold):**
> **Stop losing sales to slow POS.**

**Voiceover (energetic):**
> "Your restaurant deserves a POS that keeps up with the rush."

---

## PROBLEM (5–12 sec) — 7 sec

**Visual:** Montage — slow checkout, queue forming, customer looking impatient, paper receipts everywhere, orders scribbled on paper.

**Text on screen:**
> Clunky. Offline. Outdated.

**Voiceover:**
> "Clunky systems. No offline mode. Orders lost when the internet drops. Sound familiar?"

---

## SOLUTION (12–20 sec) — 8 sec

**Visual:** Clean shot of nResto on a tablet — smooth category tap, food grid, add to cart, payment. Fast, fluid, modern UI.

**Text on screen:**
> **nResto Mobile — Built for today's restaurant.**

**Voiceover:**
> "nResto Mobile: Fast, mobile-first POS. Works offline. Syncs when you're back online. One tap from order to payment."

---

## FEATURES (20–35 sec) — 15 sec

**Visual (split or quick cuts):**
1. **Categories + food grid** — tap, add, done (3 sec)
2. **Cart + payment** — Mobile, Card, Cash, Pay (3 sec)
3. **Orders screen** — list, chart, totals (2 sec)
4. **Thermal print** — receipt printing (2 sec)
5. **Phone + tablet** — "Works on any device" (2 sec)
6. **Background sync icon** — "Syncs in the background" (3 sec)

**Text on screen (feature callouts):**
- Dine In · Pick Up · Delivery
- Mobile · Card · Cash
- Sales at a glance
- Print receipts instantly
- Phones & tablets
- Never lose an order

**Voiceover:**
> "Dine-in, pickup, delivery. Mobile, card, cash. See your sales, print receipts, run everything from your phone or tablet. Data syncs in the background — you never lose an order."

---

## CTA (35–45 sec) — 10 sec

**Visual:** nResto logo + BOLT Fusion Tech branding. CTA button animates in.

**Text on screen (large, clear):**
> **Ready to simplify your POS?**  
> **Get a free demo → boltfusiontech.com**

**Voiceover:**
> "Ready to simplify your POS? Book a free demo at boltfusiontech.com. nResto Mobile — by BOLT Fusion Tech."

## MUSIC & TONE

- **Music:** Upbeat, modern, 120–130 BPM. Energetic but professional (think SaaS/restaurant tech).
- **Voiceover:** Confident, warm, solution-focused. Not salesy.
- **Pacing:** Fast cuts in problem section, smoother in solution/features. CTA slows slightly for clarity.

## LEAD GEN OPTIMIZATION TIPS

1. **First 3 seconds:** Must stop the scroll. Problem or bold claim.
2. **Mobile-first:** 9:16 vertical for Reels/TikTok; 1:1 for feed.
3. **Captions:** Add for sound-off viewing (80%+ watch without sound).
4. **CTA overlay:** Show URL/button from ~35 sec so viewers can tap.
5. **A/B test:** Try "Book a demo" vs "Start free trial" vs "See it in action."
6. **Retargeting:** Shorter 15-sec cut (hook + solution + CTA) for warm audiences.
